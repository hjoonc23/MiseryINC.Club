Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Misery Inc. aims to make miseryinc.club usable by people with disabilities. We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as a design target, not a claim of certification or guaranteed compliance.

Features

The site uses semantic headings and navigation, keyboard-accessible links and buttons, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts, a skip link, and reduced-motion preferences. Text is designed for readable contrast and resizing.

Limitations

Third-party websites and a future Google Form may have accessibility limitations beyond our direct control. The site has not yet undergone a comprehensive independent assistive-technology audit.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need information in another format, please contact Contact details pending. Include the page and a description of the issue, without sending sensitive personal information.
