ACCESSIBILITY
STATEMENT.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Misery Inc. aims to make its website accessible to as many people as reasonably possible.

1. Our accessibility goal

We aim to follow Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, in our design and ongoing maintenance. This is a target and not a certification or guarantee of complete conformity or legal compliance.

2. Measures we've taken

The site uses semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard-operable navigation and controls, visible keyboard focus states, scalable text, meaningful link labels, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Forms and content supplied by third parties may have different accessibility characteristics.

3. Known limitations

This site has not yet completed a comprehensive external assistive-technology audit. The Google Forms signup, when connected, is operated by a third party. There may be issues we haven't found, and we welcome specific reports.

4. Feedback and alternatives

If something is difficult to use, or you need information in an alternative format, please contact the accessibility contact to be published before signup opens. Include the page, device or browser, and the problem if you're comfortable doing so.

5. Ongoing review

We plan to review accessibility as content and features change and work to address confirmed barriers.
