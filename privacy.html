PRIVACY
POLICY.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what this early-stage media website does with personal information, including what happens if you choose to join our email list.

Before enabling signups, the operator must enter an accurate contact email and operator name in config.js, confirm that the Google Form matches this policy, and review this draft.

1. Who operates this website

Misery Incorporated (also referred to as “Misery Inc.”) is the name of an early-stage media project. The website's responsible operator is not yet published. For privacy inquiries, contact an address to be published before signup opens.

2. Information collected

The website itself does not include an operational email collection form. If the external Google Form is enabled, we intend to request your email address, an optional first name, and an explicit opt-in to receive communications. Please do not submit sensitive information.

Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, and connected network services may automatically process technical information such as IP addresses, browser and device details, and request logs under their own practices. Following social links may take you to other services with their own privacy terms.

3. How we use information

With your permission, we use waitlist details to send original content updates, company announcements, and information about potential future merchandise. Joining is free; it is not a preorder or reservation, and no purchase is required. We may use limited information to respond to a question or honor an unsubscribe or deletion request.

4. Services that help run the project

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. If configured, signups are collected through Google Forms and responses are accessible to the project operator in their configured Google account. We may use an email delivery platform in the future; if we do, this policy will be updated before use. We do not knowingly sell visitor email addresses or deploy advertising trackers on this site.

5. Cookies, local storage and analytics

We do not intentionally set optional analytics or advertising cookies on this version of the website. Your privacy preference is saved locally in your browser where supported. See our Cookie Policy for details. Visiting Google Forms or social platforms may involve cookies or tracking controlled by those providers.

6. Retention and security

Once enabled, mailing-list information will be held until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, unless we must retain limited information to comply with applicable law or maintain suppression records. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. We will limit account access and use the protections available from the service providers we choose.

7. Your choices and rights

Joining is optional. Every marketing email should provide a functional unsubscribe method. You can also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your waitlist details by contacting the operator, subject to applicable law and any lawful exceptions. Additional rights may apply depending on your place of residence and whether relevant privacy laws apply to the operator.

8. Children

This general-audience project is not intended to collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn such information was collected without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the website changes, including when a waitlist or email service becomes operational. Material changes will be reflected in the updated policy and, where appropriate, communicated before additional collection or use.

10. Contact

Privacy contact: an address to be published before signup opens.
