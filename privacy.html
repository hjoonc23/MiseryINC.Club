Publication checklist: Identify the actual operator, supply a working contact email, verify Google Forms settings, and update this policy before activating the signup. Do not publish as a finalized policy until accurate.

Who we are

Misery Inc. is a developing media project operating miseryinc.club. The operator's legal name and privacy contact must be provided before signup is activated: Contact details pending.

Information we collect

Currently this static site has no first-party account system or signup database. If our Google Forms waitlist is activated, we intend to collect your email address and, if you choose to provide it, a first name and your consent response. Google may process technical information under its own policies. Our hosting provider may process technical logs necessary to deliver the website.

Why we collect it

We use signup details to send the updates you requested, including media announcements and possible future merchandise launches, respond to requests, manage subscriptions, and comply with legal obligations. We will not treat a waitlist signup as an order or a paid membership.

Service providers and sharing

Google Forms and Google Workspace/Sheets (if configured) may process submissions for us. GitHub Pages hosts the public website. We do not intend to sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. If practices change, this notice must be revised before the change.

Retention and security

We intend to retain waitlist information until you unsubscribe, request deletion, or the list is discontinued, subject to necessary legal recordkeeping. Access should be limited to authorized personnel using secured accounts. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, and unsubscribe from promotional email using the unsubscribe method in each email or by contacting us at Contact details pending. Applicable privacy laws may provide additional rights depending on your location and our legal obligations.

Children

This signup is intended for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect waitlist information from children under 13. If we learn such information was collected, we will take steps to delete it.

International visitors

The initial waitlist is intended for U.S. residents. Additional requirements may apply if we actively solicit residents of other jurisdictions, including the European Economic Area or United Kingdom.

Changes

We may update this notice as the service develops and will update the date above. Material changes to how submitted information is used may require additional notice or consent.
